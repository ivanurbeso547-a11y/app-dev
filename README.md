# My Favorite Series: Fruits Basket (2021)

**Fruits Basket** is a Japanese manga series tells a story of an orphan girl after meeting some companies and learns about the Sohma family that's been cursed and able to heal their emotional wounds.

## While I Like This Series
I enjoy this series because i felt so emotional about this series especially Tohru, the orphan girl found a love that she always admire and his boyfriend Kyo always protects her.

## My favorite Characters
- Tohru Honda
- Arisa Uotani
- Saki Hanajima
- Yuki Soma
- Kyo Soma

## My Favorite Things About This Series
1. Lovely Romance
2. Enhancing Comedy
3. Emotional Encouragement

## Personal Rating
**Rating:** 9/10

#Conclusion
This series is honestly very light-hearted and very emotional to watch that always show loving and caring for everyone you desire and can also solve their flaws and weaknesses and able to break free.
